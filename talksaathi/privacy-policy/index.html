1. Introduction

PB Lab operates TalkSaathi, a platform connecting users with real human companions for paid voice conversations. This section restates the parties and scope covered above.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use TalkSaathi, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

  • Name or display name
  • Mobile phone number and/or email address
  • Profile photograph
  • Date of birth or age-verification information
  • Preferred languages
  • Account ID
  • Referral information, where applicable
  • Account creation date
  • Account status

2.2 Profile Information

  • Display name
  • Profile photo
  • Age or age range
  • Preferred languages
  • Other information that you voluntarily provide
Do not include passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information in your public profile.

2.3 Voice Call Information

TalkSaathi provides paid voice conversations between users and real human companions. When you make or receive a voice call, we may process:

  • User account ID
  • Companion account/profile ID
  • Call start time, end time, and duration
  • Call status
  • Calling rate
  • Coins consumed
  • Connection information
  • Technical information
  • Call quality and diagnostic information

TalkSaathi does not currently provide a built-in voice-call recording feature, and does not intentionally record or store the audio content of standard voice conversations. Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws if they independently record a conversation.

2.4 Wallet and Transaction Information

TalkSaathi uses virtual coins for paid voice calls. Our current conversion is: ₹1 = 1 TalkSaathi Coin.

We may collect and maintain:

  • Current coin balance
  • Coins purchased and coins used for calls
  • Recharge history and transaction IDs
  • Recharge amounts, refunds, and promotional credits
  • Referral bonuses, where applicable
  • Call charges
  • Transaction dates and times
  • Payment status

2.5 Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. We may receive information such as:

  • Transaction ID and payment reference number
  • Payment status
  • Amount paid and currency
  • Payment date
  • Payment method type
We do not intentionally store complete credit/debit card numbers, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, banking passwords, or similar sensitive payment credentials.

2.6 Device and Technical Information

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Network information
  • Device identifiers where permitted
  • Language and regional settings
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information

3. How We Use Your Information

PB Lab may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide TalkSaathi services and display companion profiles
  • Connect users with companions and establish voice calls
  • Calculate call duration and charges, and maintain call history
  • Manage coin balances and process recharges, payments, and refunds
  • Provide customer support and investigate complaints
  • Prevent fraud and abuse, and detect unauthorized activity
  • Moderate profiles and process reports and blocks
  • Send important service notifications
  • Improve application performance and diagnose technical issues
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with applicable laws

4. Real Human Companions

TalkSaathi provides access to real human companions. Depending on the Service, users may see limited companion information such as:

  • Display name and profile photograph
  • Age or age range
  • Languages
  • Rating
  • Online status
  • Calling rate

Companion availability may change at any time. PB Lab may verify, moderate, restrict, suspend, or remove companion profiles when necessary to maintain safety, security, and service quality.

5. Information Visible to Other Users

Depending on the features available, certain profile information may be visible to other users or companions, including:

  • Display name and profile photo
  • Age or age range
  • Languages
  • Rating
  • Online status

Do not publish information in your profile that you do not want other users or companions to see.

6. Payment Processing

When you purchase TalkSaathi coins:

  • The payment provider processes your payment.
  • The payment provider may provide a transaction/reference ID and payment status.
  • PB Lab verifies the transaction.
  • The appropriate number of coins is credited to your TalkSaathi account.

Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.
