Child Safety Standards

TalkSaathi — Operated by PB Lab

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

TalkSaathi is a voice communication platform operated by PB Lab. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our standards, how we prevent abuse, how anyone can report a concern, and how we act on reports.

1. Adults only (18+)

TalkSaathi is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. Children are not allowed to create an account, make or receive calls, or act as a companion. If we learn or reasonably suspect that an account belongs to a person under 18, we suspend the account and delete it and its associated information, except where we must keep information to report to authorities.

2. Prohibited content and behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited on TalkSaathi, in calls, profiles, display names, profile images and any other part of the service:

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Grooming, or any attempt to build contact with a minor for sexual purposes
  • Sexualising minors, including sexual role-play involving minors or content that depicts or describes minors sexually
  • Soliciting, arranging or encouraging contact with minors, online or offline
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images of a minor
  • Trafficking or commercial sexual exploitation of children
  • Any other conduct that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children

Violations lead to immediate and permanent removal from TalkSaathi, forfeiture of access to the service, and reporting to the relevant authorities.

3. How we prevent abuse

  • Age requirement of 18+ at sign-up, stated in our terms and privacy policy
  • Review and moderation of every companion profile, including display names and profile images, before and after it goes live. Companion profiles use avatar images, not real photographs of the companion.
  • In-app reporting and blocking tools available to every user
  • Review of reported accounts and suspension of accounts while a child safety report is investigated
  • Permanent bans for confirmed violations, with measures to prevent banned users from rejoining

4. How to report a concern

Anyone, whether or not they use TalkSaathi, can report a child safety concern:

  • In the app: open the companion's profile and tap the Report (flag) icon to send the profile to our safety moderation team, and tap Block to hide the profile and stop all calls with it. For any other safety concern, go to Settings → Report a Problem.
  • By email: write to career4prakash.in@gmail.com with the subject "Child Safety". Include the display name or account ID and a description of what happened. Do not attach or forward any sexual image of a child.
If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police straight away (112 in India). In India you can also report online child sexual abuse at the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal or call Childline on 1098.

5. What we do when we receive a report

  • Child safety reports are given the highest priority and reviewed as quickly as possible.
  • Accounts involved may be suspended immediately while we review the report.
  • Where a violation is confirmed, we remove the content and permanently ban the account.
  • We preserve relevant information and report apparent CSAM or child exploitation to the appropriate authorities, including law enforcement in India through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

6. Compliance with the law

TalkSaathi complies with applicable child safety laws, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. We cooperate with law enforcement agencies and respond to valid legal requests relating to child safety.
