Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

RadioBlink is a free online radio and audio player app for Android, developed by PB Lab (a sole proprietorship based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • You can use RadioBlink without an account.
  • The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads use your device's advertising ID.
  • If you choose to sign in, we store your sign-in details and a backup of your favourites, collections, your own stations and channel schedules.
  • Recordings and local music stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and backup at any time, in the app or on our website.

Information we collect

If you sign in (optional):

  • Account details – your name and email address (Google or email sign-in) or your mobile number (phone sign-in), and a user ID created for your account.
  • Your saved lists – favourite stations, collections, stations you added yourself, and channel schedules (station, time and repeat days), so they can be restored on another phone.
  • Lists you share – when you share your favourites or a collection while signed in, that list (its name and stations) is stored so the link works. Anyone with the link can view it.

When you use the app (signed in or not):

  • Device and advertising identifiers – used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads, and by Google Analytics for Firebase to count app installs and usage.
  • App activity and diagnostics – such as screens opened, app version, crashes and performance, collected through Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Approximate location – if you allow location access, the app uses it on your phone only to find your country and show stations from it. Your country may also be worked out from your SIM card or phone settings. Ad networks may also infer an approximate location from your IP address.

What stays on your phone: radio recordings, local music files, playlists of local music, listening history and settings. These are not uploaded to PB Lab.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: play stations, show stations for your country, and run schedules.
  • To sign you in and to back up and restore your lists when you choose to sign in.
  • To make shared links work.
  • To show ads in the free app.
  • To understand how the app is used and fix problems.

Service providers we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Remote Config) – sign-in, storing account backups and shared lists, and app analytics. Backups are stored in Google's Mumbai (asia-south1) region. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob – advertising. In regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. How Google uses data for ads
  • Message Central (VerifyNow) – sends the one-time SMS code when you sign in with a phone number. Your mobile number is shared with them only for this purpose.
  • Render – hosts the small RadioBlink server that checks SMS codes. It does not keep your number after sign-in, apart from short-lived sign-in records.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info) – the public station directory the app searches. Requests include search terms and your country, not your personal details.
  • Radio stations – when you play a station, your phone connects directly to that station's streaming server, which may see your IP address.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where required by law.

Permissions

  • Internet – to stream stations and use the services above.
  • Location (optional) – to find your country for local stations.
  • Microphone / audio recording – used only to record the radio stream you are listening to, saved on your phone.
  • Media and storage – to play your local music and save recordings.
  • Notifications, alarms and foreground service – to show playback controls and start scheduled stations on time.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account and backup are kept until you delete them. You can delete them in two ways:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account, your backup and the lists you have shared. Data on your phone is not affected.
  • On the web: follow the steps at pblab.in/radioblink/delete-account, or email support@pblab.in. We complete requests within 30 days.

Analytics and advertising data are kept by Google under its own retention rules. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your phone's Google settings.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Account backups can be read only by the signed-in owner. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children's privacy

RadioBlink is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will appear on this page with a new effective date.
